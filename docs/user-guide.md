## 1. Introduction

The AgriGEN system is used to manage all vehicles and fixed assets across the estate — from registering a vehicle or tractor, to scheduling maintenance, tracking depreciation, and generating reports for management.

The system is divided into two main modules:

| Module | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Fleet Management** | Vehicles, drivers, workshops, maintenance tasks, job cards, and service records |
| **Fixed Asset Management** | Machinery, equipment, buildings, and other fixed assets — including depreciation, maintenance, estimations, and disposals |

A **Reports** section is available for printing or exporting data from both modules.

---

## 2. How to Navigate the System

When you log in, you will see the **sidebar navigation** on the left side of the screen.

- The sidebar is divided into sections: **Fleet Management**, **Fixed Asset Management**, **Reports**, and **Master / Lookup**.
- Click on any menu item to open that screen.
- The section headers (e.g., "Fleet Management") are labels — they are not clickable links.
- The currently active screen is highlighted in teal.

**Top bar:** The top bar shows the system name and a notification bell (Alerts icon). A red badge on the bell means there are unread alerts.

---

## 3. Common Screen Patterns

Most screens in the system follow the same layout. Learning these patterns once means you can use every screen in the system.

### 3.1 The List View

When you open any screen, you will see a **list (table)** of existing records. Each row is one record. The columns show the most important information at a glance.

- Use the **search box** (top right of the table) to find a specific record by typing part of its name, code, or reference number.
- Use the **dropdown filters** (where available) to narrow down records by category, status, or date.
- The row count at the end of the filter bar (e.g., "5 of 12 records") tells you how many records are currently shown.
- Click **Clear** to remove all active filters and see all records again.

### 3.2 Adding a New Record

1. Click the **+ New [Record Name]** button in the top right of the screen.
2. A form panel (called a modal) will open over the page.
3. Fill in all fields marked with a red asterisk (*) — these are required.
4. Click **Save** to create the record.
5. If any required fields are missing, a red error message will appear under that field. Fix the errors and click Save again.

### 3.3 Editing a Record

1. Find the record in the list.
2. Click the **Edit (pencil) icon** in the Actions column of that row.
3. The form will open with the existing data pre-filled.
4. Make your changes and click **Save**.

### 3.4 Deleting a Record

1. Find the record in the list.
2. Click the **Delete (trash) icon** in the Actions column.
3. A confirmation box will appear asking you to confirm.
4. Click **Delete** to permanently remove the record, or **Cancel** to go back.

> **Warning:** Deleting a record is permanent and cannot be undone. Always double-check before confirming a deletion.

### 3.5 Form Fields — Types You Will See

| Field Type | How to use |
|---|---|
| Text box | Type directly into the box |
| Number box | Type a number (use decimals where needed) |
| Dropdown (select) | Click to open, then click your choice |
| Date picker | Click the field and use the calendar, or type the date in the correct format |
| Checkbox | Click to tick or untick |
| Toggle buttons | Click the button you want — it turns teal when selected |
| Textarea | Multi-line text box — press Enter for a new line |

## 4. Fleet Management Module

---

### 4.1 Dashboard

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Dashboard → Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a bird's-eye view of the entire fleet.

**What you will see:**
- **Summary cards** at the top showing total vehicles, active vehicles, vehicles overdue for service, and vehicles with expiring documents.
- **Charts** showing fleet activity and maintenance trends.
- **Recent alerts** and upcoming service tasks.

**How to use:**
- This is a read-only screen. You cannot add or edit anything here.
- Use it at the start of each day to check the status of the fleet.
- Click any alert card or summary card to be taken to the relevant screen for more detail.

---

### 4.2 Alerts

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Dashboard → Alerts

The Alerts screen shows all automatic system alerts — such as vehicles overdue for service, documents about to expire, or assignments ending soon.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all active alerts with their type, severity (High / Medium / Low), related vehicle or asset, and date.
- Alert badges are colour-coded: **red** = High, **orange** = Medium, **blue** = Low.

**How to use:**
1. Review the list regularly to stay on top of upcoming issues.
2. Click the **Mark as Read** or acknowledge button on each alert once you have taken action.
3. Use the filter at the top to view only High-priority alerts, or filter by alert type.

> **Note:** Alerts are generated automatically by the system. You cannot create alerts manually.

---

### 4.3 Master / Lookup Tables

The lookup tables define the categories and types used throughout the system. They should be set up once by an administrator before using the rest of the system.

#### Vehicle Types

**Menu:** Master / Lookup → Vehicle Types

Defines the types of vehicles in your fleet (e.g., Lorry, Tractor, Car, Van).

**Steps to add a new vehicle type:**
1. Click **+ New Vehicle Type**.
2. Enter the **Type Name** (e.g., "Pickup Truck").
3. Click **Save**.

---

#### Fuel Types

**Menu:** Master / Lookup → Fuel Types

**Steps to add a new fuel type:**
1. Click **+ New Fuel Type**.

3. Click **Save**.

---

#### Document Types

**Menu:** Master / Lookup → Document Types

Defines the types of official documents that vehicles can carry (e.g., Revenue Licence, Insurance, Emission Test).

**Steps to add a new document type:**
1. Click **+ New Document Type**.
2. Enter the **Document Type Name**.
3. Set the **Validity Period** in months (how long this document is valid before renewal).
4. Click **Save**.

---

#### Purpose Categories

**Menu:** Master / Lookup → Purpose Categories

Defines the categories of purposes that vehicles can be assigned to (e.g., Estate Transport, Staff Transport, Delivery).

**Steps to add a new purpose category:**
1. Click **+ New Purpose Category**.
2. Enter the **Category Name**.
3. Click **Save**.

---

#### License Categories

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Drivers & Operations → License Categories

**Steps to add a new license category:**
1. Click **+ New License Category**.
2. Enter the **Category Code** (e.g., "B1") and **Description**.
3. Click **Save**.

### 4.4 Vehicles

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Vehicles → Vehicles

The Vehicles screen is the central register of all vehicles in your fleet.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all vehicles showing registration number, type, brand/model, fuel type, and current status.

**Steps to register a new vehicle:**
1. Click **+ New Vehicle**.
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Registration Number** — the official plate number (e.g., WP CAB-1234).
   - **Vehicle Type** — select from the dropdown (must be added in Vehicle Types first).
   - **Brand & Model** — the make and model (e.g., Toyota Hilux).
   - **Year of Manufacture** — the year the vehicle was made.
   - **Fuel Type** — select from the dropdown.
   - **Engine Number / Chassis Number** — official identification numbers from the vehicle documents.
   - **Colour** — the vehicle's colour.
   - **Seating Capacity** — number of seats.
   - **Status** — Active or Inactive.
   - **Group & Estate** — which group/estate this vehicle belongs to.
3. Click **Save**.

**Steps to edit a vehicle:**
1. Find the vehicle in the list (search by registration number).
2. Click the edit (pencil) icon.
3. Make your changes.
4. Click **Save**.

> **Tip:** You cannot delete a vehicle that has active assignments or maintenance records. Set its status to **Inactive** instead.

### 4.5 Vehicle Documents

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Vehicles → Vehicle Documents

Tracks official documents for each vehicle (e.g., Revenue Licence, Insurance Policy, Emission Certificate).

**What you will see:**
- A list of all vehicle documents showing the vehicle, document type, issue date, expiry date, and status.
- Expired or soon-to-expire documents are highlighted in orange or red.

**Steps to add a vehicle document:**
1. Click **+ New Document**.
2. Select the **Vehicle** from the dropdown.
3. Select the **Document Type**.
4. Enter the **Issue Date** and **Expiry Date**.
5. Enter the **Document Reference Number** (e.g., policy number or licence number).
6. Upload or note the document details.
7. Click **Save**.

> **Tip:** Set up alerts for document renewal by ensuring the Document Type has a correct validity period set in the Document Types lookup.

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Drivers & Operations → Driver Assignments

Records which driver is assigned to which vehicle during a specific period.

**What you will see:**

**Steps to create a new driver assignment:**

3. Select the **Vehicle** from the dropdown.

### 4.11 Daily Running

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Drivers & Operations → Daily Running

Records the daily mileage and operational data for each vehicle.

**What you will see:**
- A log of daily entries showing vehicle, driver, date, starting odometer, ending odometer, distance travelled, and destination.

**Steps to log a daily running entry:**
1. Click **+ New Entry**.
2. Select the **Vehicle**.
3. Select the **Driver** (auto-populated from current assignment).
4. Enter the **Date**.
5. Enter the **Starting Odometer** (km reading at the start of the day).
6. Enter the **Ending Odometer** (km reading at the end of the day).
7. Enter the **Destination / Route** (where the vehicle went).
8. Enter the **Purpose** of the trip.
9. Add **Notes** if required.
10. Click **Save**.

> **Tip:** The system calculates the distance automatically from the odometer readings.

### 4.19 Service Schedule (Maintenance Tasks)

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Maintenance & Service → Service Schedule

This is the central screen for scheduling all maintenance work — for vehicles, agricultural machines, and heavy equipment.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all scheduled maintenance tasks with reference code, asset, workshop, date, mode, cost, and task type.

**Steps to schedule a new maintenance task:**
1. Click **+ New Task**.
2. Fill in the **Asset & Workshop** section:
   - **Asset Type** — select whether this task is for a Motor Vehicle, Agricultural Machine, or Heavy Equipment.
   - **Vehicle / Asset** — select the specific vehicle or asset (the options change based on the asset type selected).
   - **Workshop** — select the workshop (only workshops matching the asset type are shown).
3. Fill in the **Task Details** section:
   - **Task Type** — for Motor Vehicles: choose "Vehicle Service" or "Maintenance". For other asset types, this is automatically set to "Maintenance".
   - **Date** — the scheduled date of the work.
   - **Mode** — choose Scheduled, Preventive, or Breakdown.
   - **Total Cost (Rs.)** — the estimated or actual cost.
4. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** Once a task is saved, the Asset Type cannot be changed. To correct an asset type, delete the task and create a new one.

**Steps to edit a maintenance task:**
1. Find the task in the list using the search box or filters.
2. Click the edit (pencil) icon.
3. Update the fields (Asset Type is locked in edit mode).
4. Click **Save**.

### 4.20 Work Orders (Job Cards)

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Maintenance & Service → Work Orders

Work Orders (also called Job Cards) record the actual workshop jobs raised against a maintenance task. They assign technicians, record start/end times, and track labour costs.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all job cards with their reference code, linked maintenance task, asset, workshop, date, status, and total cost.

**Steps to create a new Work Order:**
1. Click **+ New Work Order**.
2. In the **Maintenance Task** section, select the **Maintenance Task** this job card is for (a summary card will appear showing the task details).
3. Fill in the **Job Card Details** section:
   - **Job Card Date** — the date the job starts.
   - **Start Time / End Time** — when work begins and ends.
   - **Status** — Open, In Progress, or Completed.
4. In the **Technicians** section, add the technicians working on this job:
   - Click **+ Add Technician**.
   - Select the technician and enter their hours worked.
   - Repeat for each technician.
5. Add any **Notes** about the work to be done.
6. Click **Save**.

**Steps to update a Work Order status:**
1. Find the job card in the list.
2. Click the edit icon.
3. Change the **Status** (e.g., from Open to In Progress, or from In Progress to Completed).
4. Click **Save**.

### 4.21 Maintenance Records

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Maintenance & Service → Maintenance Records

Maintenance Records are the detailed completion records for Maintenance-type tasks. They log exactly what was done, what parts were used, and the final cost.

> **Important:** Only tasks with Task Type = "Maintenance" can have a Maintenance Record. "Vehicle Service" tasks use the Vehicle Service screen instead.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all maintenance records with task reference, date, asset, workshop, description, and cost.

**Steps to create a new Maintenance Record:**
1. Click **+ New Record**.
2. In the **Maintenance Task** section:
   - Select the **Maintenance Task** from the dropdown. A yellow summary card will appear showing the asset, workshop, and task date.
3. Fill in the **Record Details** section:
   - **Date** — the date the maintenance was completed.
   - **Cost of Service (Rs.)** — the total service cost.
   - **Description** — describe what was done (e.g., "Engine oil change, air filter replacement, brake inspection").
4. In the **Parts / SKUs Used** section (optional), add any parts or materials that were used:
   - Click **+ Add Part / SKU**.
   - Select the **SKU** (part code) from the dropdown.
   - Enter the **Quantity** used.
   - Enter the **Unit Cost**.
   - Select the **Source**: "From Workshop" if the part came from workshop stock, or "Direct (Item Issue)" if it was issued directly.
   - If a part was removed and returned to the workshop, tick **Has removed / replaced item** and fill in the removed item details.
5. Click **Save**.

> **Tip:** The "Parts Total" amount at the bottom of the parts section updates automatically as you add items. This is separate from the "Cost of Service" — make sure both are filled accurately.

---

### 4.22 Vehicle Service

**Menu:** Fleet Management → Maintenance & Service → Vehicle Service

Vehicle Service records are used specifically for "Vehicle Service" type tasks — full services, oil changes, and scheduled servicing. Unlike Maintenance Records, they also track odometer readings and schedule the next service.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all vehicle service records with vehicle, service date, odometer, next service date, and service status (On Track / Due Soon / Overdue).

**Steps to log a Vehicle Service:**
1. Click **+ New Service**.
2. Select the **Maintenance Task** this service is linked to.
3. Select the **Vehicle**.
4. Select the **Workshop**.
5. Fill in the **Service Details**:
   - **Service Date** — when the service was done.
   - **Odometer Reading** — the km reading at time of service.
   - **Next Service Date** — when the next service is due.
   - **Next Service Odometer** — the km reading at which the next service is due.
   - **Cost Amount (Rs.)** — the service cost.
   - **Notes** — any observations or work done.
6. Add any **Parts / SKUs** used (same process as Maintenance Records).
7. Click **Save**.

**Service Status colours:**
- **Green (On Track)** — next service is more than 30 days away.
- **Orange (Due Soon)** — next service is within 30 days.
- **Red (Overdue)** — next service date has passed.

### 5.3 FA Categories

**Menu:** Fixed Asset Management → Fixed Assets → FA Categories

Defines categories within each fixed asset type (e.g., under "Agricultural Machine": Tractor, Harvester, Irrigation Pump).

1. Click **+ New Category**.
2. Enter the **Category Name**.
3. Select the **Fixed Asset Type** this category belongs to.
4. Enter the **Default Useful Life** (in years) — used for depreciation calculations.
5. Enter the **Default Depreciation Rate** (%).
6. Click **Save**.

---

### 5.4 Assets Register

**Menu:** Fixed Asset Management → Fixed Assets → Assets Register

The Assets Register is the master list of all fixed assets owned by the estate — excluding motor vehicles (which are managed in the Vehicles screen).

**What you will see:**
- A list of all fixed assets with their asset code, name, type, category, purchase date, purchase cost, and current net book value.

**Steps to register a new fixed asset:**
1. Click **+ New Asset**.
2. Fill in the **Asset Information** section:
   - **Asset Code** — a unique code for this asset (e.g., FA-0009). This must be unique.
   - **Asset Name** — a descriptive name (e.g., "John Deere 5075E Tractor").
   - **Fixed Asset Type** — select the type (e.g., Agricultural Machine).
   - **FA Category** — select the category (options update based on type selected).
   - **Serial Number** — the manufacturer's serial number.
   - **Brand / Model** — make and model of the asset.
3. Fill in the **Purchase Details** section:
   - **Purchase Date** — when the asset was purchased.
   - **Purchase Cost (Rs.)** — the original purchase price.
   - **Supplier** — who the asset was purchased from.
   - **Invoice Number** — the purchase invoice number.
4. Fill in the **Depreciation Settings** section:
   - **Depreciation Method** — Straight Line or Reducing Balance.
   - **Useful Life (years)** — how many years the asset will be in use.
   - **Residual Value (Rs.)** — the expected value at end of useful life.
5. Fill in the **Location** section:
   - **Group & Estate** — where this asset is located.
   - **Location Description** — specific location on the estate.
6. Click **Save**.

### 5.6 Depreciation

**Menu:** Fixed Asset Management → Fixed Assets → Depreciation

This screen shows the current depreciation schedule for all assets — the calculated depreciation for each period (usually monthly or annual).

**What you will see:**
- A list of all depreciation entries showing asset, period, opening book value, depreciation amount, and closing book value.
- Filter by year or asset to find specific entries.

**How depreciation is calculated:**
- The system automatically calculates depreciation based on the method, useful life, and purchase cost set in the Assets Register.
- **Straight Line**: the same amount is depreciated each period.
- **Reducing Balance**: a percentage of the current book value is depreciated each period.

This screen is read-only. To post depreciation, use the **Post Depreciation** screen.

---

### 5.7 Post Depreciation

**Menu:** Fixed Asset Management → Fixed Assets → Post Depreciation

Used by finance staff to officially post (confirm) depreciation for a given period, updating the net book value of all assets.

**Steps to post depreciation:**
1. Go to **Post Depreciation**.
2. Select the **Period** (month and year) to post depreciation for.
3. Review the list of assets and their calculated depreciation amounts.
4. Verify the amounts are correct.
5. Click **Post Depreciation**.
6. A confirmation dialog will appear — click **Confirm** to proceed.

> **Warning:** Posting depreciation is a permanent financial action. Once posted, the period cannot be re-posted. Always review the depreciation amounts carefully before confirming.

---

### 5.8 Depreciation History

**Menu:** Fixed Asset Management → Fixed Assets → Dep. History

Shows all previously posted depreciation runs, including who posted them, when, and the total amount posted.

**How to use:**
1. Filter by year or asset to find specific entries.
2. This screen is read-only.

### 5.9 Asset Maintenance

**Menu:** Fixed Asset Management → Fixed Assets → Asset Maintenance

Records maintenance work done on fixed assets (machinery, equipment, etc.) This is separate from the Fleet maintenance screens, which are for vehicles.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all asset maintenance records with maintenance code, asset, workshop, date, status, and cost.

**Steps to log asset maintenance:**
1. Click **+ New Maintenance** (or navigate to the New Maintenance form).
2. Fill in the **Asset** section:
   - **Fixed Asset** — select the asset from the register.
   - **Workshop** — select the workshop doing the work.
3. Fill in the **Maintenance Details**:
   - **Maintenance Code** — a unique reference code.
   - **Date** — when maintenance was done.
   - **Description** — what work was performed.
   - **Total Maintenance Cost (Rs.)**.
   - **Status** — Active, Completed, or Cancelled.
4. Add **Line Items** (parts or services) as needed.
5. Click **Save**.

---

### 5.10 Estimations

**Menu:** Fixed Asset Management → Fixed Assets → Estimations

Estimations are cost estimates created before maintenance work begins on a fixed asset. Once approved, an estimation can be converted into an actual Asset Maintenance record.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all estimations with their code, asset, workshop, date, total estimated cost, and status (Pending / Approved / Rejected / Converted).

**Status colour coding:**
- **Yellow (Pending)** — waiting for approval.
- **Green (Approved)** — approved, ready to convert.
- **Red (Rejected)** — rejected, will not proceed.
- **Blue (Converted)** — has been converted to a maintenance record.

**Steps to create a new estimation:**
1. Click **+ New Estimation**.
2. Fill in the **Estimation Details**:
   - **Estimation Code** — a unique reference code (must be unique).
   - **Fixed Asset** — select the asset.
   - **Workshop** — select the workshop.
   - **Estimation Date** — today's date or the date of estimate.
   - **Group & Estate** — location details.
3. Add **Line Items** — the parts and labour costs being estimated:
   - Click **+ Add Item**.
   - Select the **SKU** (part/service code).
   - Enter **Quantity**, **Unit Cost**, and **Estimated Cost**.
   - Repeat for each item.
4. Click **Save**. The estimation is saved with **Pending** status.

**Steps to approve or reject an estimation:**
1. Find the estimation in the list.
2. Click the edit icon.
3. Change the **Status** to Approved or Rejected.
4. Click **Save**.

**Steps to convert an Approved estimation to a Maintenance Record:**
1. Find the approved estimation in the list.
2. Click the **Convert** button (arrow icon) in the Actions column.
3. A confirmation will appear. Click **Convert** to proceed.
4. The estimation status changes to **Converted** and a new Asset Maintenance record is created automatically.

### 5.11 Asset Disposal

**Menu:** Fixed Asset Management → Fixed Assets → Asset Disposal

Records the disposal of fixed assets that are no longer in use — whether sold, scrapped, written off, or transferred.

**What you will see:**
- A list of all asset disposal records with asset code, asset name, disposal method, disposal date, disposal value, and status.

**Disposal Methods:**
- **Sale** — asset was sold to a third party.
- **Scrap** — asset was scrapped (no sale value).
- **Write Off** — asset is written off the books.
- **Transfer** — asset transferred to another estate or entity.

**Steps to record an asset disposal:**
1. Click **+ New Disposal**.
2. Fill in the **Asset** section:
   - **Fixed Asset** — select the asset being disposed. Only active assets are shown.
3. Fill in the **Disposal Details**:
   - **Disposal Code** — a unique reference code.
   - **Disposal Date** — when the disposal occurred.
   - **Disposal Method** — select from the dropdown.
   - **Disposal Value (Rs.)** — the amount received (enter 0 for scrap/write-off).
   - **Buyer / Recipient** — who received the asset (for Sale or Transfer).
   - **Reason / Notes** — explanation for the disposal.
4. Fill in the **Financial Details**:
   - **Net Book Value at Disposal** — the asset's book value on the disposal date (auto-calculated from depreciation records).
   - **Gain / Loss on Disposal** — automatically calculated (Disposal Value minus Net Book Value).
5. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** Once an asset is disposed, it will no longer appear in the Assets Register as Active. It cannot be assigned for maintenance or depreciation after disposal.

## 7. Tips & Frequently Asked Questions

---

**Q: What should I set up first before using the system?**

Set up the Master / Lookup tables first, in this order:
1. Vehicle Types, Fuel Types, Document Types, Purpose Categories, License Categories
2. Fixed Asset Types, FA Categories
3. Workshop Categories, Workshops
4. Technician Categories

Only after these are set up can you register Vehicles, Drivers, and Fixed Assets properly.

---

**Q: I cannot find a vehicle / asset in a dropdown. Why?**

Check the following:
- The vehicle/asset has been registered in the Vehicles or Assets Register screen.
- Its status is **Active** (inactive records do not appear in dropdowns).
- The asset type matches what you are looking for (e.g., a workshop set to "Motor Vehicle" will not appear when scheduling maintenance for a machine).

---

**Q: I entered something wrong — can I correct it?**

Yes. Find the record in the list and click the **Edit (pencil) icon** to correct any field. Some fields are locked after saving (e.g., Asset Type on a maintenance task) — if this needs to change, delete the record and create a new one.

---

**Q: I accidentally deleted a record. Can I get it back?**

No. Deletions are permanent. Always double-check before clicking **Delete** in the confirmation dialog.

---

**Q: A task does not appear in the Maintenance Records dropdown. Why?**

The Maintenance Records screen only shows tasks with **Task Type = "Maintenance"**. Tasks set to "Vehicle Service" use the **Vehicle Service** screen instead.

---

**Q: I posted depreciation for the wrong period. What do I do?**

Contact your system administrator. Posted depreciation periods are locked and cannot be reversed from within the system.

---

**Q: How do I print a report?**

Open the report screen and click the **Print** button at the top right. Your browser's print dialog will open. Use **Ctrl+P** (Windows) as an alternative shortcut.

---

**Q: How do I export data to Excel?**

Click the **Export CSV** button on any report screen. This downloads a `.csv` file which can be opened directly in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
